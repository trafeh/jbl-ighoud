⏰ 2. Time Settings (I3dadat L-Waqt)

Hna kat-t7kkem f kifach l-waqt kaydouz w ch-hal kat-stghreq koul 24 sa3a:

Current Time float (Range 0.0 - 24.0)
L-waqt l-7ali f l-l3ba b l-a3dad l-3achriya.
Chnou kaydir: Kay-stori sa3a d daba. Matlan 7.0 kat3ni 7:00 AM, 14.5 kat3ni 2:30 PM (14h30), w 21.75 kat3ni 9:45 PM. L-game mprogagrmé kaybda f 7.0 dima.
Day Duration In Minutes float (Default: 5.0)
Ch-hal mn dqiqa f l-waqi3 (Real-world minutes) khassha bach idouz nhar kamel f l-l3ba.
Chnou kaydir: Ila derti 5.0, nhar kamel dial 24 sa3a f l-l3ba ghaydouz f 5 d-dqayeq dial besseh. Ila bghiti nhar itewwel, dir 10.0 (10 dqayeq) wlla 15.0.
Time Multiplier float (Default: 1.0)
Sor3a dial douz l-waqt (Fast-Forward).
Chnou kaydir: 1.0 howa l-3adi. Ila bghiti t-zreb l-waqt f Play Mode bach tchouf l-ghroub w l-lil bzerba, dir 5.0 wlla 20.0.
Is Paused bool (Checkbox)
T-waqqaf l-waqt (Pause).
Chnou kaydir: Ila kochetiha, l-waqt kay-tbloqa f sa3a li raki fiha ma kaye-bqa-ch it7rrek, mziana ila bghiti t-focusi 3la chi mar7ala f l-l3ba.
Current Day int (Default: 1)
Nemra dial nhar (Compteur).
Chnou kaydir: Kaybda f Day 1. Koulma katkemmel 24 sa3a w twsel l 00:00, kaytzad nhar jdid automatic (Day 2, Day 3...).

☀️ 3. Celestial Lighting (L-Chams w L-Qmer)

Had l-options homa li 7ellou l-mochkil dial "blue then black" bach maykhelliwch l-dnya twlli kahla f marra:

Sun Light Light (Directional Light)
Dow l-ra'issi dial l-3alam (Directional Light f l-hierarchy).
Chnou kaydir: Kay-attachi Directional Light dial l-scene. Had l-light kayakhoud dawr l-chams b nhar w dawr l-qmer b l-lil.
Control Sun Rotation bool (Checkbox)
Douran dial l-chams w l-qmer m3a sa3a.
Chnou kaydir: B nhar kayle3 l-chams mn ch-cherq (10°), katwsel l-zenith (75°) f dhohr, w katghreb f l-gherb. B l-lil, kayakhoud dawr l-qmer w kaybqa dima dow dayer mn l-foq bach l-ard matwllich kahla!
Control Sun Light bool (Checkbox)
T-beddal l-couleur w l-intensity dial dow.
Chnou kaydir: Dow kaytbeddel f koul taniya: f sbah kaykoun dhbi byed, f dhohr pure white, f l-ghroub limouni w 7mer, w f l-lil kaywlli zreq fathi hadé (silvery-blue moonlight).
Control Ambient bool (Checkbox)
Dow l-bî'a l-3amm (Ambient Light).
Chnou kaydir: Kayt7kkem f RenderSettings.ambientLight. Muhimm bzaf 7it kay-filli d-dlam dial l-lil b-dow zreq hadé bach l-perso w l-ard ibano w matkounch 3amyak d-dlam.
Control Fog bool (Checkbox)
D-dbaba l-jawwiya (Atmospheric Fog).
Chnou kaydir: Ila knti m-active Fog f RenderSettings, had l-option katbeddel couleur d-dbaba automatic bach twali f nafs loun l-ghroub wlla loun sma.
Sun Azimuth Offset float (Degrees, e.g. 50°)
Zawia dial masar l-chams f l-ofsoq.
Chnou kaydir: Kat-wejjah mnin katle3 l-chams w fin katghreb bnisba l-jbel w l-terrain dialek (Compass rotation).

✨ 4. Continuous Skybox Transition (Smooth Blending)

Hna kayn l-mizan l-kbir li kaykhlli sma t-beddel "chwya b chwya":

Chnou kaydir: Had l-option katkhlli l-blending shader Custom/SkyboxBlendedCubemap idir fondu (dissolve) S-Curve bin les skyboxes. Ma kaynch plateau w ma kaynch freeze: koul taniya katmchi fiha, sma katbeddel chwya b chwya mn zarqa l-werdiya l-limouniya l-zarqa l-layliya!

Waqt d t-te7yid dial Global Illumination w les réflexions.
Chnou kaydir: Koul 2 twani kay-dir update l DynamicGI.UpdateEnvironment() bach l-m3adin w l-ma dial Suimono y-3ekso l-loun jdid dial sma bla ma i-tiyye7 les FPS dial l-game.
